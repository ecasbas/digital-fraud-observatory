# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique claims and evidence without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats and victim blaming are not acceptable. Do not use contributions for spam, retaliation or unsupported accusations.

Make space for corrections and contributors with different levels of technical knowledge. Disclose relevant affiliations.

Report conduct concerns privately to emilio@desenmascara.me. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or ban repeated or serious misconduct. Decisions should be proportionate and explainable without disclosing private reports.
